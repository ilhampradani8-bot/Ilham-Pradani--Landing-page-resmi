1. RINGKASAN EKSEKUTIF

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

EasyMarket didesain untuk menjadi platform marketplace pintar terintegrasi dengan jaringan logistik digital mandiri. Fokus utama proyek ini adalah menyederhanakan rantai pasokan global, memotong inefisiensi alokasi pengiriman barang, dan memberdayakan pelaku usaha mikro, kecil, dan menengah (UMKM) agar memiliki akses langsung ke pasar internasional secara efisien menggunakan kecerdasan buatan.

EasyMarket is designed as an integrated smart marketplace backed by a proprietary digital logistics network. The platform's primary goal is to simplify global supply chains, eliminate distribution inefficiencies, and empower micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) with direct, AI-optimized access to international trade networks.

2. VISI UTAMA

2. PRIMARY VISION

Menjadi pionir platform dagang-logistik pintar terpadu yang menyatukan UMKM lokal dengan rantai distribusi digital global secara mudah, efisien, dan transparan melalui implementasi ekosistem logistik otonom terdesentralisasi.

To pioneer a unified, smart trade-logistics platform that integrates local MSMEs into global digital distribution lines easily, efficiently, and transparently through decentralized autonomous logistics ecosystems.

3. MISI STRATEGIS

3. STRATEGIC MISSION

Dalam mewujudkan visi kemitraan dagang kami, EasyMarket mengusung tiga misi strategis:

To realize our trade partnership vision, EasyMarket drives three strategic missions:

Demokratisasi Niaga: Mendorong demokratisasi e-commerce dan menyediakan gerbang akses pasar terpadu bagi semua skala usaha tanpa biaya perantara yang mahal.

Democratizing Commerce: Powering e-commerce democratization and providing unified market gateways for all business scales without high intermediary costs.

Automasi Pergudangan: Menerapkan automasi pergudangan dan pemetaan rute pengiriman logistik pintar berbasis analisis efisiensi kecerdasan buatan.

Warehouse Automation: Enforcing warehouse automation and smart delivery routing based on artificial intelligence efficiency analysis.

Escrow Non-Kustodian: Mengintegrasikan sistem pembayaran escrow aman non-kustodian guna mempercepat perputaran arus modal kerja bagi mitra dagang.

Non-Custodial Escrow: Integrating secure, non-custodial escrow payments to accelerate working capital turnover for merchants.

4. KEUNGGULAN INTI

4. CORE ADVANTAGES

Platform EasyMarket berdiri di atas tiga keunggulan bersaing utama:

The EasyMarket platform stands on three core competitive advantages:

Optimasi Rute AI: Proses penentuan kurir dan perumusan rute logistik instan yang menyesuaikan kepadatan lalu lintas dan volume kiriman secara otomatis.

AI Route Optimization: Instant courier dispatch and logistics pathfinding that adapts to real-time traffic and parcel volumes automatically.

Sistem Kepercayaan: Protokol pembayaran rekber (escrow) dan kemitraan asuransi logistik bawaan yang menjamin keselamatan barang kiriman 100%.

Trust Ecosystem: Native escrow payment protocols and built-in cargo insurance partnerships that guarantee 100% parcel safety.

Integrasi Skala Global: Koneksi API terbuka dengan penyedia jasa kurir nasional, regional, maupun ekspedisi kargo laut/udara internasional.

Global Scale Integration: Open API connections with local, regional, and international air/sea shipping freight services.

5. TEKNOLOGI PENCOCOKAN AI

5. MATCHING TECHNOLOGY

Infrastruktur kami memproses jutaan data permintaan barang dan kapasitas armada kurir secara real-time. Dengan algoritma heuristik canggih, EasyMarket memangkas inefisiensi rute logistik hingga 35%, mengoptimalkan biaya distribusi per unit barang secara signifikan untuk menekan harga jual akhir.

Our infrastructure processes millions of trade demand signals and courier capacities in real-time. Using advanced heuristic algorithms, EasyMarket slashes logistics route inefficiencies by up to 35%, significantly lowering per-unit distribution costs to offer better end consumer prices.

6. KEPATUHAN NIAGA

6. TRADE COMPLIANCE

Kami mengintegrasikan kepatuhan penuh terhadap regulasi perdagangan elektronik (e-commerce) dan perlindungan data privasi konsumen. Kerangka ini memastikan model operasional non-kustodian tetap kokoh serta siap berintegrasi dengan perbankan nasional maupun regional secara aman.

We integrate full compliance with e-commerce regulations and customer data privacy standards. This framework ensures our non-custodial operational model remains resilient and fully ready to integrate with national and regional banking safely.
